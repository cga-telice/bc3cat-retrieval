description: Close a research sprint — write its report against the frozen design, with provenance
argument-hint: <sprint-id>
---

Close sprint **$1** on `research/synthetic-oe`.

1. **Read the frozen design** `docs/synthetic-oe/sprints/SPRINT_$1_DESIGN.md` and its
   amendments, `SPRINT_$1_AMENDMENTS.md` (D-045; S4 and earlier keep theirs in the design). The report is written against that design, not against what the
   work turned into.
2. **Write the report** at `SPRINT_$1_REPORT.md` from `_TEMPLATE_REPORT.md`.
3. **Every number carries its provenance stamp** `{run_id, config SHA, code commit,
   query-set SHA-256}`, and comes from `runs/` — read it, do not recall it. A number you
   cannot stamp does not go in the report. This is not a formality: the previous submission
   failed on exactly this.
4. **Tables and figures live in `results/`**, generated by a committed script. The report
   points at them. Budget: 15 KB. If you are over it, you are pasting output that belongs in
   `results/`.
5. **Fill "What is now known to be wrong."** It is the most valuable section and the easiest
   to leave empty. A sprint that falsified nothing either got lucky or did not look.
6. **State hypothesis movement plainly** — supported, refuted, unresolved, not addressed.
   No hedging verbs.
7. **Declare every deviation** from the frozen design, matched to its amendment entry. An
   undeclared deviation is a defect.
8. **Propose decisions** raised or superseded, for `DECISIONS.md`.
9. **Then run `/audit $1` in a fresh session.** Do not audit your own work here — you
   already believe the numbers. The sprint is not `done` until the audit returns
   `PASS` or `PASS WITH FINDINGS` and the findings are resolved or recorded.
10. **Update** the registry row, its log, and `STATE.md`.
