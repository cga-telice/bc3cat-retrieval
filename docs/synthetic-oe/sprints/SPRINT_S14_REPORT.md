**Population and reading.** Dev only: 2,331 BT queries, 2,276 item-scored (55 D-033 golds), 38 concepts at item
level, 40 at parent level. Each query is paired with the same arm's STACKED-P v2 query of the same leaf and with
its identity result on the gold. Tie-free is the reading, as run beside it, concept-clustered intervals; the
query-level interval is printed and never read. The oracle arms († in every table) read `parameters`, which on BT
hold the gold's own values: they are bounds, in no test. No BT query is flagged by the D-004 detector, so the clean
reading equals the tie-free one. The F-BT-1-free reading drops the 1,493 «de hormigón» queries (A4), which are
**every dev query of four OEB concepts** (`OEB020$`, `OEB030$`, `OEB230$`, `OEB290$`), and reads 783 queries on 34
concepts: a different, smaller population, not the same one with a defect removed. None of the five base arms is
below the floor (`dense_e5`'s identity is 0.5167 on these leaves). The S11 arms' identity is their `texto_u` run
(A3); the other arms' their `texto` run.

## Findings

**1. The ordering is partly preserved: six of the seven pairs in P hold on BT, four of them with wider margins
than on STACKED-P and two with narrower ones; the seventh reverses sign, on the four F-BT-1 concepts** (T2, T8).
P holds the seven contrasts STACKED-P v2 supports (ColBERT above every arm; `dense_e5` and `bge_m3_dense` above
BM25; `dense_e5` above `dense_es_hiiamsid`); `bge_m3_dense` − `dense_es_hiiamsid` (+0.0598, Holm 0.1308) and
`dense_es_hiiamsid` − `bm25_unigram` are not in it. On BT, ColBERT − `bm25_unigram` is +0.5144 [+0.4093, +0.5603]
against +0.3289, `bge_m3_dense` − `bm25_unigram` +0.2893 against +0.0673, `dense_e5` − `bm25_unigram` +0.2006
against +0.0902 and ColBERT − `dense_e5` +0.3139 against +0.2388 — wider; ColBERT − `bge_m3_dense` +0.2251 against
+0.2616 and ColBERT − `dense_es_hiiamsid` +0.2466 against +0.3214 — narrower; all six *supported* (Holm p ≤
0.0128). `dense_e5` − `dense_es_hiiamsid` goes from +0.0826 [+0.0189, +0.2054] to **−0.0672 [−0.1221, +0.0139]**:
*not supported*, not contradicted, so **B1 reads *partly preserved*** (not every pair in P positive; none
contradicted). Without the 1,493 F-BT-1 queries — without four OEB concepts — it reads +0.0166 [−0.1150, +0.1241],
*not supported* with a positive point estimate, and the rule returns *preserved* on that 34-concept population; on
the four concepts `dense_es_hiiamsid` reads 0.3972 against `dense_e5`'s 0.2860 (T10). Token and concepts cannot be
told apart: the token covers every dev query of those concepts, and STACKED-P damages the arms most on the same
leaves (T10's δ_SP). Not in P: `dense_es_hiiamsid` − `bm25_unigram` +0.2678 on BT, `dense_e5` − `bge_m3_dense`
reverses (+0.0228 → −0.0888 [−0.1463, −0.0526]), `bge_m3_dense` − `dense_es_hiiamsid` +0.0215. BT reproduces the
gap between ColBERT and the rest and between the dense arms and BM25; the order among the three weaker dense arms
is not a property the two renderings share.

**2. The method contribution holds on a rendering no LLM saw** (T3, T8). B2 is *supported*: K2 − ColBERT
**+0.2197 [+0.1511, +0.2594]** on BT (K2 0.8983, ColBERT 0.6785), against +0.3118 on the same leaves under
STACKED-P v2. Descriptive: R2 − `bm25_unigram` +0.6815, K2 − R2 +0.0527 [+0.0370, +0.0867], F1 − ColBERT −0.0213
[−0.0554, +0.0019]; by fraction, K2 − ColBERT +0.0952 [−0.0125, +0.1747] below 0.5 and +0.2700 at 0.5.
**K2's own accuracy is nominally higher on BT than on its identity**: 0.8783 → 0.8983, δ_BT +0.0200
[−0.0506, +0.0610] (the concept interval contains 0); the gain sits in the 0.5 bin (+0.0619 [+0.0000, +0.1061])
and on OEB (+0.0545 [−0.0076, +0.0957]), neither interval clear of 0, and is absent on the 97 queries where an
axis label precedes a value variant (1.0000 on both). This is a descriptive lead for S12's
registration (the extractor reads the BT phrasing of OEB values at least as well as the catalogue's), not a result.

**3. H1 on BT: the gap widens for every base arm; discrimination falls more than identification for four of
five** (T4, T8). W1 is *supported* for all five (BM25 +0.1645, ColBERT +0.3117, `bge_m3_dense` +0.5343,
`dense_e5` +0.1362, `dense_es_hiiamsid` +0.5176); W2 for four, *not supported* for `bm25_unigram` (−0.0548 [−0.1127,
+0.0794]), whose parent accuracy falls 0.9979 → 0.4393: BM25 loses the concept, as under stacking (S7, D-050).
ColBERT's gap goes 0.0008 → 0.3124 while its parent level holds (1.0000 → 0.9912).

**4. BT is the gentler rendering for every base arm, and the lexical arm is damaged almost as much by it** (T5,
T8, T1). B4 is *supported* for all five: δ_BT − δ_SP from +0.0535 [+0.0286, +0.1087] (`bm25_unigram`) to +0.3137
(`dense_es_hiiamsid`). But `bm25_unigram`'s item accuracy is 0.1641 on BT against 0.1106 on STACKED-P and 0.9007
on the identity: δ_BT −0.7366. The damage concentrates on the four F-BT-1 concepts: on their 1,493 queries BM25
reads 0.0345, on the other 783 0.4112 (T10); whether the token or those families do it is not identified, since
the two coincide, and STACKED-P damages BM25 on the same leaves as much (δ_SP −0.9290 against −0.5252).
ColBERT keeps 0.6785 (retention 0.6790), `bge_m3_dense` 0.4534, `dense_es_hiiamsid` 0.4319, `dense_e5` 0.3647.

**5. Strata** (T6–T9, descriptive). The 0.5 bin holds 1,511 of 2,331 queries on 12 concepts; the 55 D-033
exclusions all fall in the > 0.5 bin. Pure BT (178 queries, 17 concepts) reads as the rest: BM25 0.0824, ColBERT
0.7422, K2 0.8529. On S9's coverage axis BT sits in B2 (1,871 of 2,331 queries); OEB holds 1,605 queries on 12
concepts, the other subchapters have ‡ concept counts.

**6. The oracle arms as bounds** (T1). `tfidf_phrases_replace` 0.9809 on BT against 0.1898 on STACKED-P,
`oracleparams` 0.9666 against 0.1744: the `parameters` asymmetry in numbers; in no test, in no manuscript contrast.

**7. Robustness.** 21 of 23 registered readings are *supported* tie-free and 20 are robust across the four
readings; the three that are not all involve `dense_e5` and the F-BT-1-free reading (`dense_e5` − `bm25_unigram`,
`dense_e5` W1 and W2 read *not supported* on the 783-query, 34-concept subset). The as-run readings are identical
in category to the tie-free ones; the D-004 clean reading is the tie-free one.

## What is now known to be wrong

1. **That a gentler rendering spares the lexical arm.** `bm25_unigram` keeps 0.1641 of its 0.9007; the loss
   concentrates on the four concepts where one token substitution covers every query (item 4). B4's "gentler" is
   about the dense arms and ColBERT, not bag-of-words item accuracy.
2. **That P would be the fragile part of B1.** P is 7 of 10; the six pairs with ColBERT or BM25 hold on BT. The
   fragile reading is the order among the three weaker dense arms.
3. **That an arm's identity run is its ceiling on BT.** K2 reads 0.8983 on BT against 0.8783 on its `texto_u`
   identity; the "identity ceiling" framing does not transfer to the LLM arm without a stage decomposition.
4. **That the F-BT-1-free reading is a cleaner reading.** The 1,493 affected queries are every dev query of 4
   concepts, so the subset is a 34-concept population of 783 queries, not the same one with a defect removed;
   its three non-robust readings (`dense_e5`) are a loss of mass, and the token is not separable from those
   families.

## Deviations from design

Three, declared after the audit in [`SPRINT_S14_AMENDMENTS.md`](SPRINT_S14_AMENDMENTS.md): **A2** P was fixed
after the freeze and after the 18 non-S11 BT runs existed (freeze 07:13Z, runs 07:14–07:40Z, P 07:43Z, A1 07:44Z);
P reproduces from the pre-sprint STACKED-P v2 runs under an independent seed, but "before any BT result was read" is
unverifiable; runs stamp HEAD at save time, so K2 stamps `6a0effb` and the family cache `d9c4d62`, with no code
difference between the stamps. **A3** the S11 arms' identity is `texto_u`, not the design's `texto` (no such run
exists); no registered test reads it. **A4** F-BT-1 requires the token to be gone: 1,493 queries on four concepts,
not the design's substring count on five; no reading changed category.
