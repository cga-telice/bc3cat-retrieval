**Population and reading.** Dev only: 2,331 BT queries, 2,276 item-scored (55 D-033 golds), 38 concepts at item
level, 40 at parent level. Each query is paired with the same arm's STACKED-P v2 query of the same leaf and with
its identity result on the gold. Tie-free is the reading, as run beside it, concept-clustered intervals; the
query-level interval is printed and never read. The oracle arms († in every table) read `parameters`, which on BT
hold the gold's own values: they are bounds, in no test. No BT query is flagged by the D-004 detector, so the clean
reading equals the tie-free one; the F-BT-1-free reading drops the 1,498 «de hormigón» queries, which sit in 5
concepts, and reads 778 queries on 33 concepts. None of the five base arms is below the floor (`dense_e5`'s
identity is 0.5167 on these leaves).

## Findings

**1. The ordering is partly preserved: six of the seven pairs in P hold on BT, with wider margins than on
STACKED-P; the seventh reverses sign and is carried by the F-BT-1 queries** (T2, T8). On STACKED-P v2 the base
arms order ColBERT > `dense_e5` ≈ `bge_m3_dense` > `dense_es_hiiamsid` ≈ `bm25_unigram`, and P holds the seven
contrasts that ordering supports. On BT, ColBERT − `bm25_unigram` is +0.5144 [+0.4093, +0.5603] against +0.3289
on STACKED-P, ColBERT − `bge_m3_dense` +0.2251, ColBERT − `dense_e5` +0.3139, ColBERT − `dense_es_hiiamsid`
+0.2466, `bge_m3_dense` − `bm25_unigram` +0.2893, `dense_e5` − `bm25_unigram` +0.2006, all *supported* (Holm p
≤ 0.0128). `dense_e5` − `dense_es_hiiamsid` goes from +0.0826 [+0.0189, +0.2054] on STACKED-P to **−0.0672
[−0.1221, +0.0139]** on BT: *not supported*, not contradicted, so **B1 reads *partly preserved*** by the design's
rule (every pair positive and at least half supported). Without the 1,498 F-BT-1 queries the same contrast reads
+0.0206 [−0.1122, +0.1273] and the verdict is *preserved*; on the F-BT-1 subset `dense_es_hiiamsid` reaches 0.3992
against `dense_e5`'s 0.2864 (T10). The three contrasts not in P: `dense_es_hiiamsid` − `bm25_unigram` +0.2678 on
BT (+0.0076 on STACKED-P), `dense_e5` − `bge_m3_dense` reverses (+0.0228 → −0.0888 [−0.1463, −0.0526]),
`bge_m3_dense` − `dense_es_hiiamsid` +0.0215 [−0.0220, +0.1084]. What BT reproduces, then, is the gap between
ColBERT and everything else and the gap between the dense arms and BM25; the order among the three weaker dense
arms is not a property the two renderings share.

**2. The method contribution holds on a rendering no LLM saw** (T3, T8). B2 is *supported*: K2 − ColBERT
**+0.2197 [+0.1511, +0.2594]** on BT (K2 0.8983, ColBERT 0.6785), against +0.3118 [+0.1999, +0.3649] on the same
leaves under STACKED-P v2. R2 − `bm25_unigram` is +0.6815, K2 − `rules_valuenorm` +0.5442, K2 − R2 +0.0527
[+0.0370, +0.0867], F1 − ColBERT −0.0213 [−0.0554, +0.0019] (descriptive). By translated fraction K2 − ColBERT is
+0.0952 [−0.0125, +0.1747] below 0.5, +0.2700 [+0.2213, +0.3231] at 0.5 and +0.1749 on 8 concepts (‡) above.
**K2's own accuracy is higher on BT than on its identity**: 0.8783 → 0.8983, δ_BT +0.0200 [−0.0506, +0.0610]
(the concept interval contains 0); the gain sits in the 0.5 bin (+0.0619) and on OEB (+0.0545), and is absent on
the 97 queries where an axis label precedes a value variant (1.0000 on both). This is a descriptive lead for S12's
registration (the extractor reads the BT phrasing of OEB values at least as well as the catalogue's), not a result.

**3. H1 on BT: the gap widens for every base arm; discrimination falls more than identification for four of
five** (T4, T8). W1 is *supported* for all five (BM25 +0.1645, ColBERT +0.3117, `bge_m3_dense` +0.5343,
`dense_e5` +0.1362, `dense_es_hiiamsid` +0.5176); W2 for four, and *not supported* for `bm25_unigram` (−0.0548
[−0.1127, +0.0794]), whose parent accuracy falls 0.9979 → 0.4393: BM25 loses the concept under BT as it did under
stacking (S7, D-050). ColBERT's gap goes 0.0008 → 0.3124 while its parent level holds (1.0000 → 0.9912).

**4. BT is the gentler rendering for every base arm, and the lexical arm is damaged almost as much by it** (T5,
T8, T1). B4 is *supported* for all five: δ_BT − δ_SP from +0.0535 [+0.0286, +0.1087] (`bm25_unigram`) to +0.3137
(`dense_es_hiiamsid`). But `bm25_unigram`'s item accuracy is 0.1641 on BT against 0.1106 on STACKED-P and 0.9007
on the identity: δ_BT −0.7366. The damage is F-BT-1's: on the 1,498 «de hormigón» queries BM25 reads 0.0357, on the
other 778 0.4113 (T10). ColBERT keeps 0.6785 (δ −0.3207, retention 0.6790), `bge_m3_dense` 0.4534,
`dense_es_hiiamsid` 0.4319, `dense_e5` 0.3647.

**5. Strata** (T6–T9, descriptive). The 0.5 bin holds 1,511 of 2,331 queries on 12 concepts and the 55 D-033
exclusions all fall in the > 0.5 bin (243 of 298 scored). Pure BT (neither list, 178 queries, 17 concepts): BM25
0.0824, ColBERT 0.7422, K2 0.8529 — the thin stratum reads as the rest. On S9's coverage axis BT sits in B2 (1,871
of 2,331 queries, coverage 0.70–0.85), where S9 placed most single renderings; BM25's δ_BT there is −0.7881 and
ColBERT's −0.3387. OEB holds 1,605 of 2,331 queries on 12 concepts; the other subchapters have ‡ concept counts.

**6. The oracle arms as bounds** (T1, T2). `tfidf_phrases_replace` reads 0.9809 on BT against 0.1898 on STACKED-P,
`oracleparams` 0.9666 against 0.1744, `bm25_unigram_params` 0.6872 against 0.1840: the `parameters` asymmetry in
numbers. They enter no test and no manuscript contrast on BT.

**7. Robustness.** 21 of 23 registered readings are *supported* tie-free and 20 are robust across the four
readings; the three that are not all involve `dense_e5` and the F-BT-1-free reading (`dense_e5` − `bm25_unigram`,
`dense_e5` W1 and W2 read *not supported* on the 778-query, 33-concept subset). The as-run readings are identical
in category to the tie-free ones; the D-004 clean reading is the tie-free one.

## What is now known to be wrong

1. **That a gentler rendering spares the lexical arm.** BT translates half the sentences and keeps the label
   sentences, yet `bm25_unigram` keeps 0.1641 of its 0.9007: one systematic token substitution («de hormigón» for
   «hormigonada», 1,498 queries) is enough. B4's "gentler" is a statement about the dense arms and ColBERT, not about
   bag-of-words item accuracy.
2. **That P would be the fragile part of B1.** The design expected few supported pairs; P is 7 of 10 and the six
   that involve ColBERT or BM25 hold with wider margins on BT. The fragile reading is the order among the three
   weaker dense arms, which the two renderings do not share.
3. **That an arm's identity run is its ceiling on BT.** K2 reads 0.8983 on BT against 0.8783 on its own `texto_u`
   identity; the extractor reads the back-translated phrasing of OEB values at least as well as the catalogue's.
   S11's "identity ceiling" framing does not transfer to the LLM arm without a stage decomposition on BT.
4. **That the F-BT-1-free reading is a cleaner reading.** The 1,498 affected queries sit in 5 concepts, so the
   subset is a 33-concept population of 778 queries, not the same population with a defect removed; its two
   non-robust readings (`dense_e5`) are a loss of mass, not a correction.

## Deviations from design

None in substance. A1 records P as the design requires (fixed before any BT result was read; the 18 non-S11 BT
runs existed, unread, when P was fixed — the design allows the runs to be made first). Two commits moved HEAD
during the batch (`23a162f` → `6a0effb`), so the three S11-arm runs stamp a later commit than the other 18; the
difference under `src/` and `configs/` is the S14 generator itself. The S11 arms' identity is their `texto_u` run
(S11), as the design states; the other arms' is their `texto` run.
