## Findings

All item-level Acc@1 on dev unless stated; CIs are paired query-level bootstrap, 10,000
resamples, seed `20260917`. Tables with concept-clustered CIs and Holm/BH p in `results/S2/`.

**1. Identity** ([`identity.md`](../results/S2/identity.md)). `bm25_unigram_params` 0.9994
[0.9992, 0.9996] query-level, [0.9976, 1.0000] clustered; `bge_m3_colbert` 0.9861
[0.9849, 0.9873] query-level but **[0.9463, 1.0000] clustered — straddling 0.98**;
`bm25_unigram` **0.8870** [0.8837, 0.8903], [0.7996, 0.9242] clustered; structured rules 0.7708,
valuenorm 0.8014. Parent level ≥ 0.9975 for all five. The retrieve/metrics code path returns
0.9994 on the same query table, so the harness reads its target back.

`bm25_unigram`'s 4,003 misses are broken down in
[`identity_misses.md`](../results/S2/identity_misses.md), derived, not counted by hand:

| class | n | |
|---|---:|---|
| `duplicate_texto` | 388 | gold and winner have byte-identical `texto` — undecidable from text |
| `identical_tokens` | 274 | differ only in characters the analyzer drops (`<`, `>`, `=`) — an exact score tie |
| `gold_superset` | 1,579 | the gold's tokens contain the winner's, and it still loses |
| `rank1_superset` | 680 | the winner's tokens contain the gold's |
| `both_differ` | 1,001 | each has tokens the other lacks |
| `other_concept` | 81 | |

So **ties explain 662 of 4,003, not the shortfall**. The dominant mechanism is subsumption:
dropping the comparators turns the three maintenance bands `i < 3 horas`, `3 <= i < 5 horas`,
`i >= 5 horas` into `{i, 3}`, `{3, i, 5}`, `{i, 5}`, one containing another; the query is
binary-weighted and those tokens are near-ubiquitous (vocabulary 682), so the shorter sibling
wins by under a point in ~430. The differing line is the maintenance band in 2,574 misses and
the conditions of execution in 575. `bm25_unigram_params` recovers them because its parameter
tokens encode the comparator (`param_banda_de_mantenimiento_i_ge_5_horas`) — including all 388
duplicate-`texto` leaves, which it can only separate with information absent from the `texto`
(D-010 note). 776 leaves (292 groups) of the corpus share a `texto` with another leaf: an upstream
defect, not a retrieval one.

**There is no 1.0 to reach.** 776 dev leaves share their `texto` with a sibling, so a `texto`-only
method expects at most **0.9863** identity Acc@1 under a uniform draw inside each duplicate group.
Every group is inside one concept — `OEA050$` 192 groups of 3, `OEG050$` 100 of 2 — so this caps
the item level only; **parent-level Acc@1 keeps its 1.0**. Upstream confirmed the cause
(2026-09-17): those two concepts' TEXTO templates never reference one of their parameter axes, so
its values render identically; the RESUMEN does separate them, which is why the delivered
deduplication, keyed on the (resumen, texto) pair, kept them.
ColBERT sits on that ceiling: 484 of its 492 misses are duplicate-`texto` leaves, 8 are not, and
all 492 have the gold tied with rank 1 on score (float16 quantisation), so no rule reading the
score can fix them ([`tiebreak.md`](../results/S2/tiebreak.md)). `bm25_unigram`'s 4,003 misses
include 532 such leaves and 3,471 others, which is the real shortfall.

**3. Structured vs BM25** ([`structured_vs_bm25.md`](../results/S2/structured_vs_bm25.md)).
"CI entirely above 0" is **false in all 8 contrasts**, query-level and clustered. On
`unit_conversion` structured trails `bm25_unigram_params` by −0.373 [−0.461, −0.284] and
`bm25_unigram` by −0.127 [−0.216, −0.039]. On `num_to_text` it leads by +0.016 [−0.035, +0.067]
and +0.003 [−0.048, +0.054]: point estimates, not an overtaking. The two structured variants
score identically on both L1 slices; they differ on 70 other `single_texto` queries.

**4. Why structured does not overtake** ([`structured_stages.md`](../results/S2/structured_stages.md)).
Re-derivation reproduces rank 1 on 100 % of queries in all six runs. On both L1 slices Stage 1
is 1.000, but **no query recovers every axis** (0/204, 0/315): the rewritten value is not in the
schema, the extractor abstains on that axis, and Stage 3 returns the sub-family. For
**`structured_rules`** gold is in the match on 94.1 % / 99.4 % of queries (for
`structured_valuenorm`: 98.5 % / 100 %) and, in both variants, is **never the unique match**. The
residual item accuracy is which matched key sorts first — Stage 3 returns keys in lexicographic
order.

**5. Failures** ([`failures.md`](../results/S2/failures.md)). 30 of 80 `unit_conversion` and 30 of
284 `num_to_text` BM25-params misses, seeded: **60/60 genuine rendering effects**. Mechanisms:
numerals spelled out (the sibling-separating token is gone); converted decimals tokenising to
high-idf `0` / `m`; `unit_conversion` applied to the maintenance band in tube families;
composite dimensions losing their size token. Co-present, not causal: the thousands-dot
artefact (4) and token doubling, D-004 (10).

**6. Stacked** ([`stacked.md`](../results/S2/stacked.md)), reported, not gated: item Acc@1
params 0.1789, unigram 0.1071, ColBERT 0.4038, rules 0.1142, valuenorm 0.1269. BM25 parent
Acc@1 falls to 0.3967 / 0.2931; ColBERT 0.9837, structured 0.9722. **The by-dose strata of that
table are suspended**: upstream reports (2026-09-17, `bc3cat-dataset` `synthetic`) that the
stacked set's `modification_count` / `modification_types` overstate what the TEXTO actually
shows — rewrites of RESUMEN-only variables were counted when the phrase reappeared through
another variable, ≈1,800 of them. The texts, and therefore every Acc@1 above, are unaffected;
any reading *by dose* waits for the corrected `texto_modification_count` /
`texto_modification_types` fields, delivered upstream on 2026-09-17 and not yet taken in here.
**SINGLE is verified clean** (4,439 of 4,439 items carry exactly one modification visible in the
TEXTO), so the L1 slices of findings 2, 3 and 7 — selected by `modification_count == 1` — hold as
measured; only the stacked by-dose strata move.

**7. ColBERT under L1, with intervals** ([`colbert_vs_bm25.md`](../results/S2/colbert_vs_bm25.md)),
computed after the audit (F3). ColBERT − BM25, item level: `num_to_text` +0.667
[+0.606, +0.724] vs params and +0.654 [+0.594, +0.711] vs unigram; `unit_conversion` +0.328
[+0.230, +0.422] vs unigram — but **+0.083 [−0.010, +0.177] vs tuned BM25, clustered
[−0.148, +0.333]**, which includes 0. Three of the four contrasts invert; against the tuned arm
under `unit_conversion`, S2 has no evidence of an inversion.

**8. What candidate order is worth** ([`tiebreak.md`](../results/S2/tiebreak.md)), all five
methods, after the audit (F6). Replacing tie order with a uniform draw inside the rank-1 tie
moves item Acc@1 by ≤ 0.007 for the BM25 arms and ColBERT on every query set (identity: −0.0000),
because their tie sets are small and the gold wins about as often as chance would give it. It
costs the structured arms −0.052 / −0.059 at identity and −0.039 / −0.042 under L1, where 64 % of
queries are tied and the mean tie holds 19 keys. Lexicographic order is therefore a real
component of the structured numbers and not of the others — but it had to be measured on all
five to say so.

## What is now known to be wrong

1. **The rules pipeline is not the method H3 predicts will win.** It cannot read a rewritten
   value: Stage 2 matches schema literals, so every L1 rendering abstains on exactly the axis
   that separates siblings. Its L1 item accuracy is lexicographic luck (finding 4).
2. **Its identity is not a ceiling either.** Stage 3 orders tier 1 by `item_key`, so structured
   item Acc@1 exceeds its unique-gold rate (0.7708 vs 0.6747 identity) by ordering alone. Measured
   against a uniform tie-break the ordering is worth +0.052 / +0.059 at identity; for the BM25
   arms and ColBERT the same measurement gives ≤ 0.007 (finding 8).
3. **The previous study's 0.903 structured figure** was measured with a Stage-3 comparison that
   cannot match decimal-comma values (1,080 OEB leaves; 1,640 on OE).
4. **`bm25_unigram_params` is not a deployable baseline on synthetic queries**: its query tokens
   come from the record's parsed `parameters` (D-010 note).
5. **`bm25_unigram` cannot reach the identity ceiling on OE**: the analyzer erases comparators.
6. **"The harness runs end to end on OE" (S1) held for BM25 only.** The JSON input path, the
   ColBERT builder and the dense retrievers all failed at OE scale (A4).
7. **`unit_conversion` is not only unit conversion**: in tube families it rewrites the
   maintenance band (`i >= 5 horas` → `5 horas o más`).
8. **Parametric collapse is not universal**: under stacking BM25 loses the concept too (0.3967).
9. **`normalize_text` reads a synthetic `0.015` as a thousands group** (D-010 note).
10. **776 OE leaves (292 groups) share their `texto` with another leaf**, all intra-concept
    (`OEA050$`, `OEG050$`), because those TEXTO templates never reference one parameter axis. No
    `texto`-only method can reach 1.0 item-level identity on OE; the expected ceiling is 0.9863.
    Parent level is unaffected. `bm25_unigram_params` clears them only through parameter tokens,
    i.e. off-text information.
11. **The stacked set's modification counts overstate the dose visible in the TEXTO** (upstream,
    2026-09-17): ≈1,800 counted modifications are not in the query text. Acc@1 figures stand;
    by-dose readings do not (finding 6).
12. **ColBERT's identity misses are all score ties**, so its 0.9861 measures tie order, not
    discrimination (finding 1).
