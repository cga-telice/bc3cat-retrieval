`bm25_unigram` is the one arm the previous study reported at a different operating point (`k1`=0.80). Run at **that** point on OE it reaches **0.0148** [0.0136, 0.0161] against 0.0142 at 0.60/0.35 — so the row is not an artefact of the point we carry.

## What this says, and what it does not

**It does not say the previous number was wrong.** It says the protocol yields a different result on this chapter, and S3 measured why. Two findings settle the mechanism between them:

1. **The operating point transfers** (D-036). A 25-point sweep over both BM25 variants finds `k1`=0.60/`b`=0.35 is the argmax in 4 of 6 cells, and the two exceptions are noise (p = 0.845 and 0.417, with near-symmetric flip counts). Tuning is not where the difference lives.
2. **OE is 15 points less verbatim than OEB** (D-037). `resumen→texto` lexical coverage is **79.38 %** on OE against **94.09 %** on OEB, measured with the definition that reproduces the review analysis's own figures to 0.01 pp. The previous headline was measured on the chapter where the task is most nearly lexical containment.

**Carry the confound.** Chapter and regime are not separable here: OEB is 67.6 % of the corpus and the only chapter the previous study used. The honest claim is *"does not replicate on OE"* — the two chapters pose different tasks.

**And the table now has a `dense_es_hiiamsid` row**, which the previous submission never printed although it described the model as one of its seven neural configurations.
