`bm25_unigram` is the one arm the previous study reported at a different operating point (`k1`/`b` = 0.80/0.35). Run at **that** point on OE it reaches **0.0148** [0.0136, 0.0161] (n = 34,646 scored) against 0.0142 at 0.60/0.35 — so the row is not an artefact of the point we carry.

A headroom above 1.0 means the summary does better than the arm's own target text: `bge_m3_sparse` finds the concept from `resumen` at 0.9508 against 0.8845 from its own `texto`. Its identity figure is depressed by the unnormalised scoring `ceiling.md` diagnoses, so its own target is not an upper bound for it; why a summary suffers less from that was not tested.

## What this says, and what it does not

**It does not say the previous number was wrong.** It says the protocol yields a different result on this chapter. S3 made two measurements that bear on why, and **neither is a causal account**: the design keeps overlap descriptive in this sprint and fixes the replication as a reference, not a contrast.

1. **Tuning was not tested on `resumen`.** The sweep ran on `texto`, `single_texto` and `stacked_texto`. There, 0.60/0.35 is the unique argmax in 3 of 6 cells and ties exactly for it in one more (`bm25_unigram_params` / `texto` with 0.80/0.35), and the cells where it is beaten gain nothing detectable (+0.0014, p = 0.8507; +0.0049, p = 0.4250). On `resumen` itself there are two points, 0.0142 and 0.0148 for `bm25_unigram` at 0.60/0.35 and 0.80/0.35. Failing to detect a gain is not showing there is none, and the swept sets are not the regime of the gap, so whether tuning explains part of it is **open**.
2. **OE's summaries are less verbatim copies of their targets than OEB's.** `resumen→texto` lexical coverage is **79.38 %** on OE dev (n = 35,422) against **94.10 %** on OEB (n = 47,514, the population the review analysis measured, 22,305 of whose pairs belong to OE test concepts), with the definition that reproduces the review analysis's own figures. That is a difference between two populations that also differ in chapter, corpus and split — a description, consistent with the gap, and not a measurement of what caused it. A within-OE comparison belongs to S6.

**Carry the confound.** Chapter and regime are not separable here: OEB is the largest chapter of the corpus and the only one the previous study used. The honest claim is *"does not replicate on OE"*.

**And the table now has a `dense_es_hiiamsid` row**, which the previous submission never printed although it described the model as one of its seven neural configurations.
